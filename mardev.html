About Market Development

The role that grows Kahf's presence in the market by turning awareness into real product trial, ultimately creating loyal customers, through various marketing activities.

Key Responsibilities

01

Market Activation

Plan and run activities like events, sponsorships, and product sampling to build awareness and drive trial.

02

Consumer Conversion

Guide consumers from first discovery to becoming loyal users through targeted marketing programs.

03

Market Monitoring

Track market trends, new technology, and competitor moves to keep every activity relevant.

04

Strategic Planning

Create and adjust plans based on past results and real conditions on the ground.

05

Operational Support

Manage the operational side — stock and budget planning, briefs, decks, and product support.

What We're Looking For

  • Open to all majors, no specific background required
  • Organizational, event, or community experience is a plus
  • Understands the product and personal care industry
  • Up to date with market trends, new technology, and seasonal moments
  • Good communication, negotiaton, and team management skills
  • Able to analyze data and create marketing strategies
  • Creative, adaptable and able to multitask
